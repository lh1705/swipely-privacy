Swipely – brisanje računa

Aplikacija Swipely · TAVIRA DIGITAL, obrt za digitalne usluge, vl. Helena Hunski · Zadnje ažurirano: 1. studenoga 2026.
HrvatskiEnglish

Način 1: u aplikaciji (odmah)

  1. Otvori Swipely i prijavi se.
  2. Na početnom zaslonu dodirni ikonu zupčanika (Postavke).
  3. Na dnu dodirni Obriši račun i potvrdi.

Račun i svi podaci brišu se odmah.

Način 2: e-mailom

Pošalji e-mail na devbynarda@gmail.com s adrese kojom si registriran/a u aplikaciji i zatraži brisanje računa. Račun i podatke obrisat ćemo u roku od 30 dana od primitka zahtjeva.

Što se briše

  • korisnički račun (e-mail adresa i podaci za prijavu)
  • sve mape, setovi i kartice
  • statistika i aktivnost učenja, pauzirane sesije
  • spremljene kartice i bilješke
  • setovi koje si podijelio/la kodom
  • status paketa i dnevni brojač korištenja AI funkcija

Podaci mogu kratko ostati u sigurnosnim kopijama našeg pružatelja usluge dok se te kopije ne prepišu. Podaci pohranjeni samo na tvom uređaju brišu se odjavom ili deinstalacijom aplikacije.

Važno: brisanje računa ne otkazuje pretplatu. Otkaži je u postavkama App Storea (Postavke → tvoje ime → Pretplate) ili Google Playa (Trgovina Play → ikona profila → Plaćanja i pretplate). Zapise o kupnji Apple, Google i RevenueCat čuvaju prema svojim pravilima i zakonskim obvezama.

Više o obradi podataka pročitaj u Pravilima privatnosti.

Swipely – account deletion

Swipely app · TAVIRA DIGITAL, obrt za digitalne usluge, vl. Helena Hunski · Last updated: 1 November 2026
HrvatskiEnglish

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Swipely and sign in.
  2. On the home screen, tap the gear icon (Settings).
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all your data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: by email

Email devbynarda@gmail.com from the address you registered with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete your account and data within 30 days of receiving your request.

What gets deleted

  • your user account (email address and sign-in details)
  • all folders, decks and cards
  • study statistics and activity, paused sessions
  • saved cards and notes
  • decks you shared with a code
  • your plan status and daily AI usage counter

Data may remain briefly in our provider's backups until those are overwritten. Data stored only on your device is removed when you log out or uninstall the app.

Important: deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or Google Play (Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions). Apple, Google and RevenueCat keep purchase records according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Read our Privacy Policy for more about how we process data.
