Swipely — Delete Account

How to delete your account

To delete your Swipely account and all associated data, send an email to:

leonarda.hunski@gmail.com

Include the email address you registered with and request account deletion.

What gets deleted

  • Your user account
  • All folders, decks, and cards stored in the cloud
  • Study statistics and activity
  • Saved cards and notes
  • Daily usage counters

Timeline

Your account and all data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of receiving the request.

Local data

Data stored locally on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the app.
