Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create flashcards?

Go to any folder, tap "New Deck," then either paste text or upload a PDF. Swipely's AI will automatically generate question-and-answer flashcards from your material.

How does the swipe study mode work?

Swipe right if you know the answer, swipe left if you don't. Cards you don't know will reappear later in the session so you can practice them again.

How do I generate a quiz?

Open any deck and select the Quiz option. Swipely's AI will create a multiple-choice quiz based on your flashcards.

How do I upgrade to Pro or Premium?

Go to Settings → Plans to see available subscription options and pricing.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings → Plans → "Restore Purchases." This re-links an existing subscription to your account if it's not showing as active.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play, not within Swipely directly:

  • iOS: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions
  • Android: Google Play Store → Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at devbynarda@gmail.com with your account email, and we'll delete your account and all associated data.
