Swipely — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

1. What data we collect

Swipely only collects data necessary for the app to function:

  • Email address — for authentication and login.
  • Flashcard and folder data — your study content, stored on your device and optionally synced via Supabase servers.
  • Study statistics — number of reviewed cards, streak, and activity (stored locally and optionally in the cloud).

2. AI data processing

When you use AI features (card generation, explanations, quizzes), the text of your cards is sent to Google Gemini AI service through our secure server. Swipely does not store or forward this data to any other third parties.

3. Data storage

All data is primarily stored locally on your device. It can optionally be synced with Supabase cloud service for backup and multi-device access. Data is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — each user can only access their own data.

4. Data sharing

Swipely does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • AI services (Google Gemini) — only when you actively use AI features.
  • Supabase — for authentication and optional data sync.

5. Sharing flashcard sets

When you share a flashcard set via a code, the card content becomes available to anyone with that code. Do not share sensitive data through this feature.

6. Data deletion

You can delete all your data by logging out and uninstalling the app. To delete cloud data, contact us at the email below or visit our account deletion page.

7. Children

Swipely is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We reserve the right to modify this policy. Changes take effect upon publication on this page.
