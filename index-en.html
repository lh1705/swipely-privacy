Swipely — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Swipely collects only data necessary for app operation:

  • Email address for authentication (via email/password or Google Sign-In)
  • Flashcard and folder data stored on your device with optional cloud sync
  • Learning statistics (card views, study streaks, quiz results)
  • Subscription status for plan management

2. AI Data Processing

When using AI features (card generation, explanations, quizzes), your study material text is sent to Google Gemini API for processing. This data is not stored by Swipely beyond the immediate request. Google's AI services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

3. Data Storage

Data is primarily stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. Optional cloud sync via Supabase uses Row Level Security — each user can only access their own data. Cloud data is stored on Supabase servers in the EU.

4. Third-Party Services

Swipely uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — authentication and optional cloud sync
  • Google Gemini API — AI-powered card generation and explanations
  • Google Sign-In — optional social login
  • RevenueCat — subscription and payment management
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Data is only sent to the services listed above for their specific purpose (authentication, AI processing, payments).

6. Sharing Flashcard Sets

When you share a flashcard set via a share code, the content becomes accessible to anyone with that code. Share codes do not include personal information.

7. In-App Purchases

Swipely offers optional subscription plans (Pro and Premium) managed through Google Play. Payment information is handled entirely by Google Play — Swipely does not collect or store payment details.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at devbynarda@gmail.com. Local data can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

9. Children's Privacy

Swipely is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: devbynarda@gmail.com
